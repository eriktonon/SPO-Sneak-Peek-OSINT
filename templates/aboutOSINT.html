school Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) Methodology

Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) is the structured collection, correlation, and analysis of publicly available data from the internet, public registries, DNS/RDAP infrastructure, certificate transparency logs, official gazettes, and developer platforms to produce actionable intelligence.

PHASE 01
Planning & Scope

Define target organization, domains, CNPJs, or handles inside an Investigation Case.

PHASE 02
Collection

Query RDAP, DNS, crt.sh, BrasilAPI QSA, Shodan InternetDB, OTX, and GitHub events.

PHASE 03
Processing

Normalize CNPJ documents, deduplicate subdomains, resolve IPs, and extract commit emails.

PHASE 04
Correlation

Pivot from domain registrant CNPJ to QSA partners, government contracts, and open CVEs.

PHASE 05
Dissemination

Export structured JSON evidence packages for legal, red-team, or compliance reporting.
