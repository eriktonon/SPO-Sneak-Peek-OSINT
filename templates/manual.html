SP — Sneak Peek OSINT Operator Field Manual

Step-by-step operational procedures for executing corporate due diligence, infrastructure surface discovery, personal & email attribution across 73 networks, Strix DAST auditing, and local neural synthesis with eriktonon/granite4.2-uncensored.

RUNBOOK 01 // CORPORATE & DOMAIN /company-recon

Company, Domain & CNPJ/QSA Investigation

Navigate to Company & Domain and submit a domain (e.g. petrobras.com.br) or a 14-digit CNPJ. For .br domains, SP automatically extracts the registrant CNPJ via Registro.br RDAP and pivots into BrasilAPI/ReceitaWS to retrieve the corporate QSA (officers & partners), share capital, CNAEs, Certificate Transparency subdomains (crt.sh), and official gazettes.

Open Company & Domain Recon
RUNBOOK 02 // INFRASTRUCTURE & CVES /server-intel

Server, IP & Threat Intelligence

Navigate to Servers & Threat Intel and submit an external IPv4 address or hostname. SP queries Shodan InternetDB (free, zero API key required) for open TCP ports, CPE technologies, and CVEs, correlates ASN/ISP geolocation, and retrieves passive DNS history from AlienVault OTX and VirusTotal v3.

Open Server & CVE Intel
RUNBOOK 03 // SOCMINT, DATING & EMAIL OSINT /personal-trace

Personal Handle, Dating & Direct Email Trace

Navigate to Personal & Social Trace and enter a username/handle, an email address, or both. SP probes 73 platforms concurrently (Tinder, Badoo, OkCupid, Bumble, Happn, OnlyFans, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, GitHub, Steam) and runs DNS MX/SPF validation, OpenPGP key lookup, GitHub commit email correlation, and breach dorks.

Open Personal & Email Trace
RUNBOOK 04 // STRIX DAST & PREVENTION /security-audit

Strix Black-Box DAST & Shift-Left Autofix

Navigate to Strix DAST & Security and enter an external domain or URL. Strix evaluates OWASP Top 10 HTTP defensive headers, TLS 1.3 cipher negotiation, exposed sensitive paths (/.env, /.git/HEAD), and DNS SPF/DMARC anti-spoofing policies, generating ready-to-apply Shift-Left code fixes.

Open Strix Security Auditor
