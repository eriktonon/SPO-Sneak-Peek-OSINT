menu_book SPO v2.0 Operator Manual

1. Company & Domain Investigation

Navigate to Company & Domain and enter a domain (e.g., itau.com.br) or a 14-digit CNPJ. For .br domains, SPO automatically extracts the registrant CNPJ via Registro.br RDAP and pivots into BrasilAPI/ReceitaWS to retrieve the corporate QSA (partners), capital social, CNAEs, Certificate Transparency subdomains, and official gazettes.

2. Server, IP & Threat Intelligence

Navigate to Servers & Threat Intel and submit an IPv4 address or hostname. SPO queries Shodan InternetDB (free, zero API key required) for open TCP ports, CPE technologies, and CVEs, correlates ASN/ISP geolocation, and retrieves passive DNS history from AlienVault OTX and VirusTotal v3.

3. Personal & Social Reconnaissance

Navigate to Personal & Social Recon and enter a username or email. SPO concurrently probes 10 developer and social platforms, inspects public GitHub push events to uncover commit author emails, checks Gravatar hashes, and builds targeted social dorks.

4. Evidence Persistence & Export

Every scan is automatically logged to the local SQLite database (data/spo.db). Create named cases in Investigation Cases to group scans and export complete JSON evidence bundles.
