STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE // SPO v2.0
RUNBOOK REFERENCE

SPO v2.0 Operator Field Manual

Operational guide for executing corporate due diligence, infrastructure surface discovery, personal identity attribution, Strix DAST auditing, and local neural synthesis with eriktonon/granite4.2-uncensored.

RUNBOOK 01 // CORPORATE & DOMAIN

Company & Domain Investigation

Navigate to Company & Domain and submit a domain (e.g. itau.com.br) or a 14-digit CNPJ. For .br domains, SPO automatically extracts the registrant CNPJ via Registro.br RDAP and pivots into BrasilAPI/ReceitaWS to retrieve the corporate QSA (officers & partners), share capital, CNAEs, Certificate Transparency subdomains, and official gazettes.

RUNBOOK 02 // INFRASTRUCTURE & CVES

Server, IP & Threat Intelligence

Navigate to Servers & Threat Intel and submit an IPv4 address or hostname. SPO queries Shodan InternetDB (free, zero API key required) for open TCP ports, CPE technologies, and CVEs, correlates ASN/ISP geolocation, and retrieves passive DNS history from AlienVault OTX and VirusTotal v3.

RUNBOOK 03 // STRIX DAST & PREVENTION

Strix Black-Box DAST & Shift-Left Autofix

Navigate to Strix DAST & Security and enter an external domain or URL. Strix evaluates OWASP Top 10 HTTP defensive headers, TLS 1.3 cipher negotiation, exposed sensitive paths (/.env, /.git/HEAD), and DNS SPF/DMARC anti-spoofing policies, generating ready-to-apply Shift-Left code fixes.

RUNBOOK 04 // NEURAL SECOPS AI

Ollama Granite 4.2 Uncensored Co-Pilot

Navigate to Granite 4.2 SecOps AI, select any recorded reconnaissance or Strix scan from the dropdown, choose an operational mode (OSINT Correlation, Red Team Surface, CVE Triage, or Due Diligence), and run local inference via eriktonon/granite4.2-uncensored.
